Loadsheet Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Loadsheet Pro is a weight & balance calculator for A320 and A330 family aircraft. This policy covers the iPad and Mac app and the web version.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data that stays on your device

Values you enter (weights, fuel, registration, flight details) and your settings (such as kg/lb and default taxi fuel) are kept only on your own device so the app can work. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Network use

When online, the app downloads the latest version of the calculator page from a public web address to check for updates. This request sends no personal information. PDFs you create are saved or shared only where you choose.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if sign-in is added in a future version, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the TestFlight “Send Beta Feedback” option in the app.
